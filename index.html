Radar Backups

A private, personal-use application operated by Zain to manage his own Google Drive files and YouTube account.

Personal use only

This application is for the owner's own accounts. It is not a public service, does not offer public registration, and does not request access to unrelated people's accounts. The Google authorization screen uses the app name Radar Backups.

Google Drive

The application supports personal file and backup management in the owner's Google Drive, including uploading, listing, downloading and verifying backups. Older backup files may be removed after successful verification, within the owner's approved workflow.

YouTube

The application uses YouTube API Services to access the owner's channel information, statistics and non-monetary analytics for personal channel management and content planning.

Video uploads, publishing and content updates are intended future capabilities, subject to the owner's authorization. The current connector supports account authorization and read-only channel and analytics checks; publishing has not yet been implemented or tested, and no unattended publishing or recurring monitoring is enabled.

Authorization and control

Google account access is granted through Google OAuth. Permissions may be broader than the operations currently implemented. The owner controls their use and can revoke access through Google Account settings. Necessary channel reports may be processed by an AI assistant to support personal planning; see the privacy policy for details.

This informational website does not host private files, channel reports, credentials or authorization tokens.

Read and accept the privacy policy before using the application. Use of the YouTube integration is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
